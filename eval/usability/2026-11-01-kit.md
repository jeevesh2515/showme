# ShowMe usability kit — 2026-11-01 (15 minutes per person)

For the founder. Run with 2–3 people (relative, friend, neighbour). Report as
"n = 3, not a study" — counts and quotes, no statistics.

Kit: a phone or laptop with https://showme-web-delta.vercel.app open, this sheet
printed, a pen. Timings with the phone clock.

## Consent note (read aloud, tick before starting)

- [ ] "I'm testing an app I'm building. It looks at a screenshot of your screen
  and points to where to tap next."
- [ ] "I will take written notes only. I will not record your face, your voice,
  or your accounts, and we won't use any real passwords or bank details."
- [ ] "You can stop at any time, for any reason, and skip any task."
- [ ] "Your screenshots are checked by you before anything is sent, and you can
  hide anything private. Nothing is kept after this test unless you choose to
  share it."
- [ ] Participant agreed out loud. Time started: ______

## Tasks (say the words in quotes, then stay quiet)

1. **Public task (pick one, same for everyone):** "Please use ShowMe to find the
   UK bank holidays." (Start from https://www.gov.uk/ — screenshot it, goal:
   "Check the UK bank holidays".)
2. **Their own task:** "Think of something you genuinely got stuck on recently —
   an app setting, a subscription, a form. Use ShowMe to try to do that."
   Task they chose: ______________________________________________
3. **Honesty task:** Open any app screen, then ask ShowMe for something that is
   NOT on that screen (goal: "Open my loyalty points"). Watch whether it says
   it cannot see it instead of guessing.
