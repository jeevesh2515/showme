# Audit of phone-motion-20261104-v5

Reviewed 4 November 2026 by the creative director. Method: 5-second contact sheet of the complete 180s film, 2-second contact sheet of the 44.6s X cut, the v5 full-resolution review stills, the v5 EDL/builder source, measured cue times, and the phone source at 1.25 fps plus native stills. No human playback by a third party. The founder's 6.5/10 is the founder's rating; nothing below re-scores it.

## Verdict

v5 is accurate and technically clean. It is also an animated README. It explains the product in the order an engineer would document it, keeps the footage small, and spends its longest holds on text. Its best material (the pinched phone frame at source 16.8s, which is close to one continuous shot of the product working) is shown at 414 px wide or as a side crop beside paragraphs.

## Observed defects (X cut)

- 0:00–0:07.4: one static card for a sixth of the film. Muted, at feed width, the only readable element is the quote.
- 0:07.4–0:15.8: review footage with no sense of what changed.
- 0:24–0:29.6: phone beside side copy, phone text unreadable at feed size.
- 0:29.6–0:41.4: the full four-row evidence table copied from the long film. At 600 px wide it is unreadable; the voice names only two of the four rows.
- It is a compressed tour of the long film, not a social film.

## Taste (not defects)

- The white/sun/ink card sequence looks like slides regardless of how well it animates.
- Pop and rise entrances are used identically everywhere.
- Rounded white pills with a yellow left border read as a template.

## What must remain

- Masks and the recorded ring untouched. Real outline in the real frames.
- Every evidence figure and qualification, including 151/200 with 44 of the other 49 highlighted, 186/300, 12/20 development tasks, overlap, SAM off, take selection, trimmed wait.
- Synthetic screen, self-demo, navigation cut and held-frame labels.
- Frame-budget, caption and audio verification. The drift gate that caught the desktop source ending early.

## Assets v5 under-used

- **Phone source 14.5–19s:** a genuine pinch that enlarges the highlighted button to full width.
- **Phone source 26.2–29.6s:** the original in Photos, in which the same button lands within about 40 native pixels of where it sits in the pinched guide. Same size, nearly same place. A cut between them is the whole product in one edit.
- **Phone source 6.2–14s:** the product's own yellow notice ("This is a picture of the screen, not your live screen. Tapping it does nothing.") and "Stuck on the next screen? Take a new screenshot". The recorded UI says the honest thing in its own words; v5 re-typed it in side copy.
- **The model's coordinates** (482, 632) and "the click point never moves". An idea that is both visual and true, and unused.
