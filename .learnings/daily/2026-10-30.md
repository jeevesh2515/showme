## How a request flows
1. You drop/paste a screenshot and type a goal on /app.
2. On-device OCR finds private text; you check and add hides on the mandatory review screen.
3. Only the redacted picture is sent to /api/v1/step; the ring + instruction appear.
4. /api/v1/refine draws the SAM outline over the box when it agrees; history stays text-only in the tab.

## 5 judge questions
1. Does the raw screenshot ever leave the device? No — only the reviewed, re-encoded image; PRIV-1 test inspects every request body.
2. What if OCR misses private text? Review is mandatory; tap/brush hides; after 15 s OCR stall it says it could not check and asks you to hide manually.
3. What if the model is wrong? One step at a time, unsure is dashed with check-first copy, "That didn't work" feeds back as failed history.
4. What does a step cost? ~$0.006 measured (ground $0.0037 + SAM $0.0025 on today's live calls).
5. Where does it fail? Cookie banners become the first step; bot-blocked sites (Royal Mail) get an honest "blocked" answer, not a guess.
